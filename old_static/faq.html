FAQ

Answers, up front.

25+ questions we hear most, answered honestly.

How is ProPresence different from other agencies?

Senior-only teams, product-grade craft, and every engagement tied to measurable business KPIs.

What size companies do you work with?

From Series A startups to enterprise brands. We qualify by ambition and fit, not headcount.

Do you offer one-off projects?

Yes — the Foundational pillar covers fixed-scope engagements like brand and website.

How fast can we start?

Most engagements kick off within 2 weeks of signed scope.

Do you sign NDAs?

Always, before we exchange any sensitive information.

What tools do you use?

Figma, Linear, Notion, GA4, Segment, HubSpot, and a modern MarTech stack tailored to each client.

Do you work with in-house teams?

Yes — we operate as an extension of your team, not a replacement.

How do you measure success?

KPIs are defined together in week one and reviewed live in a shared dashboard.

What industries do you specialize in?

SaaS, DTC, fintech, healthcare, real estate, education, creators and more.

Yes — from $10K/mo to seven-figure quarterly budgets.

Growth retainers start at 3 months; Enterprise at 6.

Do you offer equity or performance deals?

Case by case, for select high-conviction partnerships.

Can you white-label for other agencies?

Yes, under specific terms.

Do you build custom software?

Yes — full-stack product builds are part of our Enterprise pillar.

Do you do SEO?

Yes — technical, on-page, content and digital PR.

Can you help with fundraising materials?

Yes — decks, data rooms, positioning.

Do you do PR?

Selectively, tied to launches and thought leadership.

Fully remote, with global team members.

SOC2-aligned processes, SSO, principle-of-least-privilege access.

Yes — workshops for in-house teams available.
